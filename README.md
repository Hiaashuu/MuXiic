## ✨ Overview

**MuXiic** is a handcrafted, high-performance Android music streaming and offline playback experience designed and engineered by **[Hiaashuu](https://github.com/Hiaashuu)**. Built entirely with **Kotlin** and **Jetpack Compose**, MuXiic blends bold editorial typography with ultra-thin accents, real-time artwork color extraction, morphing geometry, and an intelligent **Vibe & Tempo Continuation Engine** that understands what you want to hear next.

Whether you listen to **Modern Hindi, 90s Evergreen Classics, Punjabi Urban, Indie Acoustic, Desi Hip-Hop, Haryanvi, Sufi, Bhakti, English Pop, or Arabic Fusion**, MuXiic dynamically tailors every shelf, radio station, and recommendation around your personal listening habits—with zero ads, zero subscriptions, and seamless offline caching.

## 📲 Installation & In-App Updates

1. Go to the **[Releases Page](https://github.com/Hiaashuu/MuXiic/releases/latest)** and download the latest `MuXiic` `.apk` asset.
2. Open the downloaded APK on your Android device (Android 8.0 Oreo / API 26 or newer) and install it.
3. Complete the quick **Taste & Singer Onboarding** to personalize your sound engine.
4. **Future Updates:** You never need to manually visit the browser again—**MuXiic** features a built-in **OTA GitHub Release Updater** that notifies you on launch (or via *Settings → Check for App Updates*) and installs updates directly inside the app.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** Long-press the **Search** tab at the bottom to jump straight into Search with the keyboard open, or long-press any song/album card anywhere in the app for instant **Start Radio**, **Play Next**, **Like**, and **Download** options!
